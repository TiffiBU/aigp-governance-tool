

# AIGP Governance Tool

A simple web-based comparison tool for exploring key AI governance laws and frameworks.

## Why I built it

I wanted a practical way to compare different AI governance requirements while developing my understanding of responsible AI and preparing for AIGP certification.

## What it does

- Presents AI governance information in a structured comparison format
- Helps users compare key requirements across different laws and frameworks
- Makes complex governance information easier to scan and review

## What I learned

This project helped me deepen my understanding of AI governance and think about how complex policy information can be organised and presented more clearly for users.

## Why it matters to my work

My work increasingly sits at the intersection of learning, AI and quality evaluation. Building this tool helped me develop practical understanding of how governance, usability and clear information design can support responsible use of AI.
